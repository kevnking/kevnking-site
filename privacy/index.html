Privacy Policy

King SFR LLC operates kevnking.com and the phone and text messaging programs described on this site. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and what we do not do with it. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share mobile phone numbers or SMS consent with anyone for marketing purposes.

Who we are

This website, kevnking.com, is operated by King SFR LLC, a limited liability company based in Tulsa, Oklahoma. King SFR LLC purchases residential property directly from owners. Kevin King, the principal of King SFR LLC, is also a licensed Oklahoma real estate sales associate (OREC #178419) affiliated with McGraw Realtors, and brokerage services referenced on this site are provided through that brokerage.

King SFR LLC is the party responsible for the SMS and text messaging program described below, and is the sender of any text messages you receive as a result of contacting us through this site. You can reach us at 918.853.9182 or kking@mcgrawok.com.

Information we collect

We collect only what you give us and what is needed to respond to you.

Information you provide directly

  • Contact information — your name, mobile or landline phone number, email address, and preferred contact method and time, submitted through the forms on this site.
  • Property information — the address, condition, size, occupancy, and other details of a property you ask us to evaluate, along with your timeline, price expectations, and any notes you choose to include.
  • Communications — the content of emails, text messages, and voicemails you send us, and our records of those exchanges.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, we receive standard technical information when you visit: IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, and pages viewed. This is used for security and to understand which pages are useful. We do not use this information to build advertising profiles.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiry and answer questions about your property.
  • To prepare and communicate a valuation, an offer, or a listing recommendation.
  • To follow up with you about a property or inquiry you have already contacted us about.
  • To send appointment, showing, inspection, and closing-related reminders.
  • To complete a real estate transaction you have chosen to move forward with.
  • To keep records required by Oklahoma real estate law and by our brokerage.

We do not use your information for automated decision-making, and we do not sell, rent, or trade it.

SMS and text messaging

This section describes our text messaging program and how consent is handled. It applies to every message sent from King SFR LLC.

How we obtain consent

We send text messages only to people who have given us express written consent to receive them. Consent is obtained in one of two ways:

  • You check the SMS consent box on a form on this website. That box is never pre-checked, and submitting a form without checking it still allows us to contact you by phone or email.
  • You text or call us first, which begins a conversation you initiated.

Consent to receive text messages is never a condition of any purchase, offer, listing, or service. You can use every part of this site and receive an offer on your property without ever agreeing to receive a text message.

What messages you will receive

Messages relate to the property or inquiry you contacted us about: replies to your questions, follow-up about an offer or valuation, scheduling for showings and inspections, and reminders about closing. Message frequency varies and depends on your conversation with us; most people receive fewer than ten messages per month.

Cost, opting out, and help

Message and data rates may apply. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. You can cancel at any time by replying STOP to any message from us; you will receive one confirmation that no further messages will be sent, and we will send no more. Reply HELP for assistance, or contact us at 918.853.9182 or kking@mcgrawok.com.

Mobile information and third parties

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing with subcontractors in support services, such as customer service or messaging platform providers, is permitted. All other use case categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

To state it plainly: we do not sell, rent, lease, or otherwise transfer your mobile phone number or your SMS consent to anyone. We do not provide it to lead generators, data brokers, affiliates, marketing partners, or other real estate businesses. Consent you give King SFR LLC belongs to King SFR LLC alone and is not passed to any other sender.

When we share information at all

We share personal information only in these situations, and never for anyone else's marketing:

  • Service providers we rely on to operate — our website host, form processor, email provider, and text messaging platform. They process data on our instructions and may not use it for their own purposes.
  • Transaction participants — if you choose to move forward with a sale or listing, the parties a real estate transaction requires: the title company, lender, brokerage, inspectors, and the other side's representatives.
  • Legal obligations — when required by law, subpoena, court order, or the record-keeping rules that apply to licensed Oklahoma real estate professionals.

Mobile phone numbers and SMS consent data are excluded from all sharing described above except where a service provider is required to deliver the message you asked for.

How long we keep information

We keep inquiry and property information for as long as needed to respond to you and, where a transaction results, for the period Oklahoma real estate record-keeping rules require. If you opt out of text messages, we retain a record of that opt-out permanently so that we do not message you again. You can ask us to delete other information at any time.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect the information you send us, including encrypted transmission of form submissions. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please do not send Social Security numbers, bank account numbers, or other sensitive financial identifiers through the forms on this site.

Your choices and rights

  • Stop text messages — reply STOP to any message, or contact us directly.
  • Stop email — use the unsubscribe link or ask us.
  • Access, correct, or delete — email kking@mcgrawok.com and tell us what you want changed or removed, and we will act on it within 30 days unless a law or our record-keeping obligations require otherwise.

We do not sell personal information as that term is defined under state privacy laws, so there is nothing to opt out of on that front.

Cookies

This site uses only the cookies needed for basic functionality and simple traffic measurement. We do not run third-party advertising cookies or cross-site tracking pixels. You can block cookies in your browser settings without losing the ability to use the site or submit a form.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has submitted information to us, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the effective date at the top of this page. Material changes to the text messaging section will be posted here before they take effect.
