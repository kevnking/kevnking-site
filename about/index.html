About

Built in
the field.

Licensed in Oklahoma since 2018. Multiple personal flips. 200+ closings. I've worked with PE groups, institutional buyers, and individual sellers across single family and multifamily.

How I got here.

I started in 2018 and haven't stopped. Rookie of the Year my first year. Built a business that spans brokerage, personal flips, and investment acquisitions.

I've worked as the acquisition and disposition arm for a private equity group, sourcing and closing portfolio deals, working directly with institutional capital. I understand how PE groups underwrite, what they need to see, and how to move deals through.

On the residential side I broker single family, income-producing properties, and portfolios. I've flipped multiple homes in Tulsa personally, acting as my own general contractor.

Beyond brokerage, I'm currently partnered on a ground-up multifamily development in Tulsa.

What drives me.

Faith first. I'm married to Paige and we have three kids. We live on four acres outside Tulsa. Real estate is how I provide and build. Not a brand, just how I work.

I'll tell you when a deal doesn't make sense. I'd rather lose a deal than mislead someone about what their house is worth or what I can pay for it.
